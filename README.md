### 🔬 Featured Research

#### 🧫 Proteogenomic Refinement of the *Entamoeba histolytica* HM-1:IMSS Reference Genome

**Master's Dissertation Research — Institute of Bioinformatics, Bengaluru | Feb–Jun 2026**

Proteogenomic analysis of *Entamoeba histolytica* using high-resolution LC–MS/MS data to identify previously unannotated coding regions and refine existing genome annotations.

**Key work:**

* Developed a **Python-based six-frame genome translation workflow** generating approximately **1.16 million predicted ORFs**.
* Analyzed **38,562 unique peptide identifications**, including **480 genome search-specific peptides (GSSPs)**.
* Performed genomic coordinate mapping, sequence analysis, and interpretation of GSSPs.
* Identified evidence supporting **41 putative novel protein-coding genes, 17 N-terminal extensions, 1 C-terminal extension, and 2 pseudogene-associated translation events**.
* Integrated genomic and proteomic datasets from **AmoebaDB** and **PRIDE**.
* Applied Python, R, Linux, genome annotation, sequence analysis, and proteogenomic data interpretation.

**Publication:**
Malshetty MB, Chowdhury S, Pawar S, et al. *Application of Proteogenomic Approaches for Refinement of the Entamoeba histolytica Reference Genome Using High Resolution Mass-Spectrometry Data.* **Acta Parasitologica (2026).**

---

#### 🧬 In Silico Evaluation of Deleterious nsSNPs in SOD1 & TARDBP Associated with ALS

Computational investigation of deleterious variants in **SOD1** and **TARDBP** associated with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis.

**Key work:**

* Retrieved and filtered variants using **NCBI dbSNP** and **UniProt**.
* Evaluated variant deleteriousness and pathogenicity using **CADD, SIFT, PolyPhen-2, PhD-SNP, SNPs&GO, and ClinVar**.
* Assessed mutation-induced protein stability using **I-Mutant 2.0, MUpro, DynaMut-2, and INPS-MD**.
* Investigated secondary structural effects and post-translational modifications.
* Generated and validated 3D protein models using **SWISS-MODEL, MolProbity, and Ramachandran analysis**.
* Performed protein–protein interaction, functional enrichment, pathway, and network analyses using **STRING, Cytoscape, GeneMANIA, KEGG, ShinyGO, and Human Protein Atlas**.

**Publication:**
Malshetty MB, Mallya S, Prabhu S. *In silico evaluation of deleterious nsSNPs in SOD1 and TARDBP genes associated with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis.* **Scientific Reports (2026).**

---

#### 🩺 Toward Non-invasive Precision: A Meta-analysis of Photoacoustic Spectroscopy in Breast Cancer

Systematic review and meta-analysis evaluating **photoacoustic spectroscopy** as an emerging non-invasive approach for breast cancer detection.

**Key work:**

* Conducted systematic literature searching, screening, data extraction, and data curation.
* Worked with **PubMed, EMBASE, and Scopus** following **PRISMA 2020** guidelines.
* Screened **291 records**, with **16 studies** included for qualitative synthesis and **6 studies** for quantitative meta-analysis.
* Contributed to pooled diagnostic accuracy analysis involving sensitivity, specificity, confidence intervals, random-effects models, and heterogeneity assessment.
* Contributed to forest plots, funnel plots, subgroup analysis, and leave-one-out sensitivity analysis using **R and the meta package**.
* Contributed to scientific writing, evidence synthesis, manuscript preparation, review, and editing.

**Publication:**
Puranika KS, Malshetty MB, Mooliyil MG, Dehury B, Mazumder N. *Toward non-invasive precision: a meta-analysis of photoacoustic spectroscopy in breast cancer.* **Lasers in Medical Science (2025).**
