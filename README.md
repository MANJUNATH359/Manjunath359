### 🔬 Featured Research

#### 🧫 In Silico Evaluation of Deleterious nsSNPs in SOD1 & TARDBP Genes Associated with ALS
_Ongoing (2025–Present)_

Functional impact and protein stability assessment of ALS-associated gene variants, combining variant-effect prediction, structural modeling, and PTM analysis.

| Layer | Technology |
|---|---|
| Variant Impact Prediction | CADD, SIFT, PolyPhen-2, PhD-SNP, SNPs&GO |
| Protein Stability Analysis | I-Mutant 2.0, MuPro, DynaMut-2, INPS-MD |
| Secondary Structure Prediction | GOR4, SOPMA |
| PTM Analysis | GPS-MSP |
| 3D Structural Modeling | SWISS-MODEL |

---

#### 🩺 Toward Noninvasive Precision: A Meta-Analysis of Photoacoustic Spectroscopy in Breast Cancer
_Published — Oct 2025_

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA-guided) evaluating the diagnostic accuracy of photoacoustic spectroscopy for non-invasive breast cancer detection.

| Layer | Technology |
|---|---|
| Methodology | PRISMA Systematic Review |
| Analysis | Statistical Meta-analysis, Effect Size Calculation |
| Domain | Diagnostic Imaging, Oncology |

📄 **Publication:** Puranika K S, Malshetty MB, et al. *Lasers Med Sci.* 2025 Oct 27;40(1):461. [doi:10.1007/s10103-025-04714-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10103-025-04714-2)
